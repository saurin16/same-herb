03 / METHODS & SOURCE

What counts as a match?

Match the preparation. Check plant part and form first. For an extract, the solvent, percentage basis, and drug-to-extract ratio can change the result. A label’s full ratio range must fit the source range.

Trace the evidence. Results point to the exact section of the EMA monograph. Coverage is limited to four preparation records. Unclear or out-of-scope labels get an explanation. View the EMA source index.
